# Labware alignment SOP

## Coordinate provenance

For each station record machine, frame, units, orientation, approach pose, intermediate pose, process pose, tolerance, source method, date, and verifier.

## Progressive calibration

1. Home or establish a verified reference only when authorized.
2. Move to a high safe approach without gripping.
3. Verify measured pose and clearance.
4. Move to an intermediate height.
5. Verify alignment visually or with an approved gauge.
6. Move to process depth at reduced speed.
7. Verify final pose and tool/labware clearance.
8. Test one empty or sacrificial position before a populated rack.
9. Expand to the remaining positions deterministically and inspect residuals.

A named `A1` is machine-scoped. Robot-rack A1 and capper-rack A1 may use different frames and schemas.
